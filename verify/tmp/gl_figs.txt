FOREIGN NATIONALS
Greenland´s international twist is growing, now representing
4.3 per cent of population total. The largest immigrant groups
are Philippines, Thai and Polish. In 2013, 15,368 Greenlandic
born lived in Denmark. In 2023, the number has risen to 17,079.
